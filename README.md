The runs will serve different purposes and I break em down here for you (and me).
Race is on Thanksgiving day so we'll officially start the plan next week giving us
eight weeks to train. The goal is to run a 24 minute 5k time averaging around an 8:00min
mile.

easy to memorize and you can start to get into a rhythm easier. Also, when we do it this way
the progression will mainly be in your pace not in the amount of time spent running. But feel
free to switch up distances or time running in the workouts using your recovery level and
what you feel would be best.

Every workout will start with a warmup and end with a cool down. I'll list what I'll be doing

## Strides

* 35 min easy conversational run, then 4 × 20 sec strides with 60–90 sec easy walk/jog between.

To be honest I have no idea what the point of these are but they lowkey sound fun. Basically
you will have an easy run akin to the long runs but at the end, you will do these 20 second
'strides' where you basically sprint. We will be looking to have a 6:25-6:40 pace on these
'strides'

## Interval Run

* 10 min warm-up, then 6 × 400 m with 90 sec recovery, then 10 min cooldown.

These are used to increase our speed, we want to be aiming to be faster than race pace during
these intervals. So I would hope that we can get to around 7:20-7:40 minute pace for each stretch.
Again use your discretion, just be aiming for speed here.

## Tempo Run

* 10 min easy, 20 min at ~8:35–8:45/mile, 10 min easy.

You'll start with a warmup and then you will have the main set which should be a 7/10 effort
just above race pace. You should feel like you are pushing yourself, but you should still
feel like you can maintain that pace for an extended period of time. Starting out it is fine to
cut the times of the warmup, cooldown and main set in half, but use your discretion. The goal
here is to teach you how to hold a hard pace for an extended period of time without having a
stop in between

## Long Run

* 45-60 minutes trying to stay in zone 2.

This is where we will build our aerobic engine. You DO NOT want to go hard on these, these
should feel very easy and you should keep your heart rate between 120bpm to 140bpm. This is
what will build our engine and improve our bodies abilities to use fat as energy rather than
using carbohydrates.

* 10    Side kicks each leg

* 10    Scoops each leg
* 30sec High knees
* 30sec Butt kicks
* 5min  Jog

## Cool Down

* 5min  Jog
* 30sec Sit and reach stretch each leg
* 30sec Butterfly hold
* 30sec Front quad stretch
* 15    Calf raises into anterior tibialis raise
